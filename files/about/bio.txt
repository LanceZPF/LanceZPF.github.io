Pengfei Zhou is a Ph.D. candidate at the National University of Singapore. He received his M.E. degree from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. He was awarded with Beijing outstanding graduate, Zhejiang outstanding graduate, CSIG outstanding master (thesis incentive), and the National Scholarship on two occasions. His research focuses on multimodal learning for real-world open scenarios, including zero-shot food detection, multimodal image editing, and multimodal large language models. He has published more than 12 papers in top-tier venues such as NeurIPS, CVPR (Oral), AAAI, IEEE TIP, and Patterns (Cell Press), with 8 as first author. He also served as a reviewer for ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR and IEEE TNNLS/TMM/TYCB, etc.
